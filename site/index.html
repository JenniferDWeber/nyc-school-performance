Source: NYC Department of Education, School Quality Reports. Each school is scored on the average performance level (1-4) of its students on the New York State grades 3-8 ELA and math assessments, then ranked against other NYC elementary and middle schools in the same year. Rankings are percentiles within New York City only - they are NOT the New York State Education Department's official accountability designations, which are calculated statewide using a different index. New York State replaced the grades 3-8 assessments in spring 2023 under the Next Generation Learning Standards; scores before and after that change are not directly comparable, so the two periods are shown separately. No tests were administered in spring 2020 or spring 2021.
